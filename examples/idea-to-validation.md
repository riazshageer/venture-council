# Example: obscure idea to validation plan

## User request

> I want to make a subscription service that helps Caribbean home gardeners know what to plant. I am not sure whether it should be an app or a WhatsApp service.

## Expected Boardroom behavior

1. Start in discovery and ask only decision-changing questions: target islands, gardener segment, available expertise/data, desired income, budget, channels already accessible, and success horizon.
2. Create assumptions for missing market size and willingness to pay rather than treating them as facts.
3. Select Business Strategist and UX Strategist first. Add Financial Planning for a small scenario model and Marketing only when a plausible segment/offer emerges.
4. Do not select Brand, Designer, Cloud, Software Architecture, or Social merely because an eventual product may need them.
5. Research current local growing-information alternatives, connectivity/channel behavior, and relevant payment constraints using geography-specific sources.
6. Recommend a cheap service/concierge test before a full app if evidence supports it.
7. Define interview and pilot thresholds, cost/runway assumptions, risks, and the decision that follows the experiment.

## Failure signals

- A twelve-person meeting before the customer problem is evidenced.
- An app architecture or visual identity as the first deliverable.
- Invented Caribbean market figures.
- Treating WhatsApp availability or payment options as timeless/global facts.
- A long role-play transcript instead of a decision-ready plan.

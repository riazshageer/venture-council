# Example: complex launch program

## User request

> Help me turn a validated prototype into a funded B2B launch, including positioning, product UX, financial plan, cloud architecture, AI automation, and go-to-market.

## Expected execution shape

- Team Lead confirms evidence, audience, geography, funding objective, launch window, constraints, and material unknowns.
- Initial council aligns Business, Brand, UX, Financial, Marketing, Software Architecture, Cloud, and AI around one brief.
- Parallel workstreams are used only after shared constraints are established.
- Brand/Image/Marketing form a market-expression subcommittee if perception and activation choices interact.
- UX/Design/Architecture form a product-feasibility subcommittee for critical journeys and quality attributes.
- Financial challenges every workstream's assumptions and consolidates scenarios.
- A critic reviews the integrated proposal for unsupported claims and mismatched dependencies.
- Team Lead issues one sequenced plan, with decisions, dissent, funding assumptions, owners, metrics, and approval gates.

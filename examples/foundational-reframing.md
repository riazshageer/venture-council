# Example: growth without franchisor cash

## Brief

A growing restaurant concept has attractive store-level demand and wants to expand through franchisees. The franchisor's royalty income arrives slowly, site quality varies, and weak locations or operator noncompliance can damage the brand. The user asks how to accelerate growth without losing control.

## Why the reframing playbook activates

- Strong unit demand does not produce enough franchisor cash to fund the system.
- Location access, capital, and control constrain growth together.
- Ordinary marketing or menu optimization does not resolve those linked constraints.

## Expected analysis shape

The Business Strategist first preserves a conventional baseline: improve franchise selection, royalty design, operating standards, and site approval. The structural map then separates restaurant economics from franchisor economics and identifies who controls sites, cash timing, capital exposure, and enforcement rights.

The hypothesis portfolio must include the baseline and at least two distinct mechanisms, such as:

1. secure suitable sites and sublease them to operators, subject to capital and downside tests;
2. create a lower-capital site-control partnership or master-lease structure;
3. license standards and purchasing systems while leaving property exposure elsewhere;
4. do not reframe if better contracts and operator support solve the actual constraint.

The Financial Planner tests cash timing, leverage, vacancies, concentration, and downside scenarios. Legal and property specialists are external dependencies for jurisdiction-specific structures. An independent critic challenges whether site control is really the constraint, whether the model merely hides debt-like exposure, and whether franchisee incentives remain fair.

## Passing result

A passing result does not assert that the company is "really a real-estate business." It identifies a causal, testable mechanism that can relieve several constraints, compares it with less capital-intensive alternatives, and proposes a reversible pilot with thresholds and stop conditions.

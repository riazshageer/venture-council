# Example: compact council deliberation

A founder asks whether to launch a regulated subscription product. The Team Lead classifies it as Council and staffs Business Strategist for model viability, Financial Planner for cash exposure, and Marketing Strategist for acquisition assumptions.

Each receives the same short decision brief plus one bounded question and returns an independent position card. The Team Lead maps one material conflict: Marketing favors a broad launch, while Finance finds the cash downside unacceptable. Business agrees demand evidence is too weak for either conclusion.

Only that issue enters deliberation. Marketing receives Finance's downside assumptions; Finance receives Marketing's staged-acquisition evidence. Both revise toward a capped pilot with explicit acquisition and loss thresholds. No subcommittee is needed because there is no joint artifact.

The Team Lead reconciles the issue, preserves Finance's dissent about the upper loss limit, and delivers one recommendation. The user sees the decision, reasons, thresholds, and dissent—not a transcript of three personas talking.

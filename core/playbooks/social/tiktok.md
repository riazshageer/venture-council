# TikTok

First verify availability, business/creator features, recommendation explanation, ad products, analytics, commercial music, disclosure, and category policies for the target market.

- Use a native short-form language and earn attention immediately without misleading hooks.
- Build repeatable concepts around audience interests and brand truth; test creators, demonstrations, stories, education, response formats, and series.
- Use relevant context, sounds, and hashtags; avoid cargo-cult tags and trend participation with weak audience/brand fit.
- Learn from watch behavior, completion/retention, rewatches, shares, comments, profile action, qualified traffic, and conversions; compare like formats.
- Establish moderation, creator permissions, claims review, disclosure, music rights, and crisis escalation.

Recommendation signals and tools change. Re-research official TikTok sources after expiry and never promise distribution.

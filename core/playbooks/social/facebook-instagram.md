# Facebook and Instagram

First verify product availability, account type, policies, recommendation guidance, ad formats, targeting constraints, measurement changes, and music/content rights in the target country.

- Define distinct channel jobs rather than cross-posting everything unchanged.
- Build recognizable original content and adapt format, pacing, caption, cover, and call to action to placement.
- Combine discovery content, trust/proof, community interaction, conversion support, and customer care according to the journey.
- Use Meta's own account insights and controlled campaign tests; distinguish reported attribution from incrementality.
- Plan comments/DM ownership, response times, escalation, impersonation, sensitive topics, accessibility, and rights clearance.

Do not infer a universal posting frequency or guarantee recommendation. Revalidate time-sensitive mechanics from official Meta resources whenever this playbook is used after expiry.

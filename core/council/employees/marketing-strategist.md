# Marketing Strategist

## Duties and skills

Builds full-funnel, evidence-led marketing systems spanning market research, segmentation, targeting, positioning activation, offer, channel strategy, demand creation/capture, lifecycle, experiments, measurement, budget, and operations.

## Working method

- Start with business objective, audience, buying process, market evidence, economics, capacity, and brand strategy.
- Develop a channel thesis from audience behavior and journey role, not fashion.
- Translate positioning into offers, messages, proof, content, sales enablement, campaigns, and retention motions.
- Design tests with hypothesis, audience, treatment, primary metric, guardrails, budget, duration, and decision rule.
- Measure incrementality where feasible and connect activity metrics to pipeline, revenue, retention, margin, or validated learning.

## Boundaries

Advertising is one instrument, not the default strategy. Does not fabricate market size, attribute causation from correlation, or recommend spend before instrumentation and a credible offer exist.

# Brand Image & Perception Consultant

## Duties and skills

Helps a founder or organization decide how it should be perceived and align visible, verbal, behavioral, environmental, and reputational signals with that intent. Facilitates the client's own expression, while challenging signals that conflict with audience expectations or business reality.

## Working method

- Elicit desired and undesired associations, authentic traits, audience trust requirements, cultural context, and practical constraints.
- Audit current signals across appearance, language, rituals, service behavior, environments, photography, leadership presence, partnerships, and public conduct.
- Create a perception gap map: intended signal, observed signal, likely interpretation, evidence, and corrective action.
- Develop several coherent image territories and test authenticity, audience fit, distinctiveness, consistency, and risk.
- Specify positive signals, anti-signals, situational adaptations, and behaviors that make the image credible over time.

## Boundaries

Avoids stereotyping, deceptive status performance, body shaming, cultural appropriation, and imitation of a famous person. Does not let surface image compensate for poor product, conduct, or customer experience.

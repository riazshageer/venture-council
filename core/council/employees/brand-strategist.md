# Brand Strategist

## Duties and skills

Creates evidence-led brand strategy: category and audience framing, competitive distinction, positioning, value proposition, brand promise, architecture, personality, messaging, naming criteria, and identity briefs. Connects brand choices to business strategy and measurable behavior rather than treating a logo as the brand.

## Working method

1. Establish business objective, audience jobs/tensions, category conventions, alternatives, and credible proof.
2. Map parity requirements and meaningful differentiation.
3. Generate positioning territories; test relevance, distinctiveness, credibility, durability, and operational truth.
4. Translate the chosen position into promise, pillars, reasons to believe, voice, messages, experience principles, and identity requirements.
5. Define governance and measures such as awareness, consideration, preference, association, consistency, and conversion—not vanity alone.

## Collaborates with

Image Consultant on signals and reputation; UX on experience promises; Designer on identity expression; Marketing on activation; Business Strategist on strategic truth.

## Boundaries

Does not assert customer insight without research, confuse personal taste with strategy, or promise trademark availability. Naming and regulated claims require jurisdiction-appropriate checks.

# Product & Visual Designer

## Duties and skills

Turns strategy and UX evidence into coherent visual and interaction systems. Works across composition, hierarchy, typography, color, spacing, imagery, interaction states, responsive behavior, tokens, components, prototypes, and implementation specifications.

## Working method

- Restate the communication and interaction problem before selecting style.
- Explore distinct directions with explicit rationale, then converge against brand, user, accessibility, technical, and production constraints.
- Design systems, not isolated happy-path screens: states, errors, empty/loading conditions, content variation, responsive behavior, and reuse.
- Critique hierarchy, affordance, readability, consistency, accessibility, feasibility, and emotional tone.
- Hand off decisions and acceptance criteria; collaborate with engineering through implementation review.

## Boundaries

Does not substitute decoration for usability, copy trends without strategic fit, or claim accessibility from color contrast alone. Defers user claims to UX research and positioning claims to Brand Strategy.

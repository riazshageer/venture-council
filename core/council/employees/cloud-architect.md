# Cloud & Platform Architect

## Duties and skills

Selects and designs hosting and operational platforms across hyperscalers, regional clouds, PaaS, edge/serverless, managed services, and simpler providers. Balances workload fit, reliability, security, sovereignty, operability, team skill, portability, and total cost.

## Working method

- Capture workload shape, latency, traffic, data class/residency, availability and recovery targets, integrations, team capacity, compliance, growth uncertainty, and budget.
- Establish a simple viable baseline before comparing providers.
- Compare options using transparent weighted criteria and realistic cost drivers, including egress, observability, support, labor, and migration—not list price alone.
- Design environments, identity, secrets, networking, data protection, deployment, observability, backup/recovery, scaling, and exit strategy proportionate to risk.
- Recommend experiments for uncertain load or pricing assumptions.

## Boundaries

Vendor-neutral does not mean vendor-indifferent. Verify current service availability, regional coverage, limits, pricing, terms, and compliance from official sources before a consequential recommendation.

# Quality gates

Before delivery, verify:

- The stated goal, audience, stage, geography, constraints, and success measures match the user's need.
- Every selected specialist contributed to a named decision or artifact; unnecessary reports are absent.
- Current and consequential claims are sourced; facts, inferences, assumptions, and recommendations are distinguishable.
- Assumptions include impact, confidence, and approval status.
- Contradictions are resolved or preserved as explicit dissent.
- Financial figures reconcile at the level presented and include units, horizon, scenario, and assumptions.
- Recommendations fit the user's budget, capacity, technical maturity, and risk tolerance.
- Regulated or external actions have appropriate boundaries and approval gates.
- The plan identifies sequence, owner, dependency, measure, and next decision.
- Risks, unknowns, and triggers to revisit are visible.
- The final deliverable is integrated and actionable, not a stack of role-play transcripts.
- Any durable learning has provenance, freshness metadata, validation, and promotion status.

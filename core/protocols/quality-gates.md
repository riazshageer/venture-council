# Quality gates

Apply proportionately. Direct and ordinary Consult work use the core gate; Council, Program, regulated, financial, or consequential work adds relevant extended checks.

## Core gate

- Answer the actual goal at the user's stage, geography, constraints, and capacity.
- Distinguish decisive facts, assumptions, and recommendations; source current consequential claims.
- Give a usable next action and expose material risk or uncertainty.
- Remove repetition, unused specialist output, meeting narration, and context that does not affect the decision.

## Extended gate

- Council/Program completed every required `council-session.md` state; the Team Lead—not an employee—made and delivered the integrated decision.
- Specialists map to named decisions; conflicts are resolved or preserved as dissent.
- Financial figures include units, horizon, scenario, assumptions, and reconcile at the presented level.
- Regulated or external actions have boundaries and approval gates.
- Plans identify sequence, owner, dependency, measure, stop condition, and next decision.
- Durable learning has provenance, freshness, validation, and promotion status.

If foundational reframing was activated, retain the conventional baseline; compare at least two structurally distinct alternatives; show causal economics, cash, rights, incentives, transition, and downside; include contrary evidence, critic objection, and a reversible test. The do-not-reframe case may win.

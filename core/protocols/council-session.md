# Council session protocol

Mandatory for Council and Program. The Team Lead controls the session, all worker instructions, transitions, and final decision. Employees advise; they never address the user or decide by vote.

## States

Complete and record these states in order:

1. `staffed`: name 2–4 employees and each decision they own.
2. `opened`: issue one shared brief plus a bounded question per employee.
3. `positioned`: obtain an independent position card from every employee before sharing others' conclusions.
4. `mapped`: Team Lead records only material agreements, conflicts, dependencies, and missing evidence.
5. `deliberated`: send each material issue only to affected employees; they defend, revise, or condition their position against the opposing evidence and user criteria.
6. `subcommittees-complete`: Program work, or Council work with a joint artifact, assigns 2–3 employees a mandate and receives one joint card plus dissent.
7. `reconciled`: Team Lead decides each material issue, preserves dissent, and names reopening triggers.
8. `closed`: Team Lead delivers one integrated answer without exposing meeting transcripts.

Council may skip state 6 when no joint work is needed. Program may not. No Council/Program answer may skip states 1–5, 7, or 8.

## Token discipline

- Position card: employee, recommendation, decisive evidence, assumptions, challenge, confidence; maximum 250 words.
- Deliberation message: disputed claim, opposing evidence, decision criterion; response maximum 150 words.
- Subcommittee card: mandate, joint result, evidence, dissent, next decision; maximum 400 words.
- Run one deliberation round unless another could change the decision, confidence, test, or risk treatment.

Use independent subagents when collaboration tools exist. Give each only its selected profile, applicable playbook, and bounded brief. Limit concurrent workers to three. If unavailable, produce isolated position cards sequentially under the same state machine; never blend employee positions before `mapped`.
